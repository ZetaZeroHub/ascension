# Ascension visual direction

The physical reference is a music sleeve opened beside a film production notebook. Visitors browse the film and its sketches in ordinary ambient light on their phones.

## Existing identity

Keep the matte blue photographic hero, the large italic Georgia Ascension title, Chinese system sans typography and generous sections. These choices were approved during the original design rounds.

## Palette

Use the existing blue-ink, warm-paper and cream identity. For new navigation, use opaque ink `oklch(0.22 0.02 235)` with warm-white `oklch(0.95 0.01 95)`. Keep yellow for the album title, not small navigation text. New diagrams use the same warm paper, muted blue, burgundy character accent and pale gold seed accent.

## Layout and copy

The closing section is an illustrated invitation to create: one clear headline, three switchable illustrated views, and direct links to the relevant instructions. SVGs are vector-only, with separate phone compositions and Chinese/English editions. Avoid identical icon cards and decorative micro-labels.

## Interaction

Use simple semantic tabs with keyboard support and explicit selection, no autoplay or new media downloads. Existing reduced-motion handling remains. The final author link is prominent and leads to https://zzh.app/.
