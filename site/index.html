先让故事成立。Give it a story.

她是红衣信使。为了让静止的城市重新流动，她带走光种，遭遇追逐，最后主动跃入深渊。不是被动获救，而是用一次选择改变世界。A courier in red takes a seed of light to awaken a frozen city. Pursued and cornered, she chooses to leap. Her decision changes the world.

短发、红衣、金色腕线固定角色；“英雄之旅”把出发、试炼、转变和归返串成因果。A short bob, red coat and gold wrist thread anchor her identity. A hero’s journey connects departure, trials, transformation and return.

同一角色与服装 → 一个明确动作 → 景别和机位 → 材质与光线 → 道具、视线与运动方向 → 避免的错误。
例如：红衣信使在断桥边握紧光种，三分之四侧面中近景，干燥哑光材质；她主动看向深井，人物保持站立，不能画成失足坠落。Consistent identity → one clear action → framing and angle → materials and light → props, eyeline and screen direction → errors to avoid.
Example: the courier grips the seed at the broken bridge, in a three-quarter medium close shot with dry matte materials. She deliberately looks into the shaft, standing upright rather than slipping.

给每个镜头写动作与运镜提示词，把参考分镜送进 ComfyUI。模型按队列逐段生成，AI 检查主体、动作、连续性和画面故障，区分可用片段、需重做片段与废片。Write motion and camera prompts for each shot, then feed the reference frames into ComfyUI. The queue generates clips one by one. AI reviews identity, motion, continuity and visual defects to select, retry or reject them.
