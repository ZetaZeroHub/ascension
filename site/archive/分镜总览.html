Restrained charcoal grey, crimson and pale gold palette. Extreme architectural scale, immaculate composition, dramatic volumetric side light, atmospheric depth, tactile silk with natural folds, subtle fine film grain. Landscape 16:9 film still, full bleed, no border, no captions, no watermark. One clear focal subject. The same original chamber, now softly illuminated with pale golden daylight. The crimson ribbon hangs relaxed above the reflecting pool, a quiet vertical mark in a vast empty space. Wide symmetrical framing.
