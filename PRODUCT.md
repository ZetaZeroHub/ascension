# Ascension

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors arriving from the author's social posts to watch a music video, explore its visual development and try making a first AI-assisted shot. The site must work equally well on a phone and a desktop. Most visitors are curious creators, not engineers.

## Product Purpose

Present Zhang Hao's visual music concept album Ascension and share its production methods. Help visitors move from watching the finished film to understanding the process and trying the public workflows. Link to the author's personal website. The four-day production record is a project-specific account, not a universal speed claim.

## Brand Personality

Album-like, candid, cinematic. The approved direction combines an avant-garde music sleeve with the panoramic opening of a film art book. Preserve the existing imagery and expressive Ascension title.

## Anti-references

Avoid technical sales copy, generic AI slogans, decorative tags, software-dashboard aesthetics and repetitive feature cards. Explain what a visitor can actually do, not just what file formats exist.

## Design Principles

- Show the work through real storyboards and understandable diagrams.
- Give each sentence a clear purpose; keep detail available without overwhelming the page.
- Keep the existing photographic hero and lightweight HTML implementation.
- Describe the public archive honestly: workflow files and optional reference images are shared; source clips and weights stay offline.
- Support Chinese and English with equally considered layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support and responsive typography already present in the site. Diagrams have text alternatives; essential instructions remain selectable HTML. Navigation must remain legible over the hero image.
